The Secret to Authentic Artisan Pizza

Published: October 2026
Category: Culinary Arts
Author: Chef Marco Rossi

Making restaurant-quality pizza at home requires high heat and patience. The most common mistake is rushing the dough fermention process. A slow 24-hour cold ferement allows flavor compounds to develop fullly, resulting in a crust that is both crispy on the outside and light and airy on the inside. Never use a rolling pin-always stretch your dough by hand to preserve crucial air pockets.
